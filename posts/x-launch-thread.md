## Post 2

When I cook just for myself: no recipe, whatever is in the fridge. If it fails, nothing is lost. And it's where I learn, where I prototype dishes I would never risk on a guest.

When I cook for others, everything changes. Every choice is made for the people at the table.

## Post 3

I'm not against vibe coding. I do it in the kitchen, and I do it in code.

But making something only you will consume is different from making something other people will depend on.

## Post 4

For years, every team asked: how much engineering discipline can we afford? Architecture, testing and documentation always competed with time and cost.

## Post 5

The catch: AI can amplify bad engineering just as easily!

So the goal isn't to constrain AI. It's to direct its implementation capability toward sustainable engineering.

## Post 6
